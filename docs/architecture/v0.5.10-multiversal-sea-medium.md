# v0.5.10 Multiversal Sea Medium

Recorded: 2026-11-16

Status: completed bounded `v0.5.10` multiversal sea medium release; scope selected by the author on 2026-11-17

## Objective

Bubble is one actually constructed and executed universe (AI-20261116-001). Until this candidate, its positive and negative seas existed only as two scalar fields inside each world. Negative-sea pressure was added by a world's own law from nowhere, a retired world's residue stayed trapped inside that world, and worlds touched each other only at generation time or through one anchor crossing. That is a forest of locally running worlds rather than one universe suspended in shared seas.

The canonical ontology ([sea-anchor-model.md](sea-anchor-model.md)) and the observer-free baseline ([sea-anchor-proof.md](sea-anchor-proof.md), Axioms O and S) require otherwise. The negative sea is the outer field of exterior pressure, and it contains residue from collapsed worlds, leaks from other bubble universes, and traces pressing in from beyond a boundary. The positive sea is the larger structured field that gives worlds placement, nearness, lineage, and support. This candidate makes both seas a shared, exact, conserved medium in which every world is placed.

## Necessity gate

1. **Author concept.** The sea-anchor model and Axioms O and S; OB-003, OB-007, and OB-011 record global sea operators as open.
2. **Contradiction if absent.** If residue from a collapsed world cannot leave that world, the negative sea cannot contain "residue from collapsed worlds" or "information leaking from other bubble universes". If sea pressure can appear from nothing, outside residue cannot be distinguished from host invention, which is exactly the distinction the model makes first-class.
3. **Observer-free contract.** Regions, placements, adjacency, exchange laws, release on retirement, and a balance certificate are all exact world structure, with no observer and no human-scale analogy.
4. **Generated by law.** Every movement of sea quantity is either an exchange law of the medium itself or the release of a retiring world, never a host or World-Will selection.
5. **Provenance and failure.** Every exchange is a causal event with causes, joint scarcity is a typed conflict that branches or stays unresolved, and the balance certificate accounts for every unit.

## 1. Program contract

An executable causal program may declare `world.seaMedium` (TypeScript: `CausalSeaMedium` in `src/bubbles/world-kernel/causal.ts`). Absence means the previous semantics, unchanged.

- `regions`: shared portions of the sea. Each has an id and exact non-negative rational `negative` and `positive` quantities.
- `placements`: every declared world, active or latent, is placed in exactly one region. Placement is positive-sea structure ("placement within a larger field").
- `adjacency`: undirected edges between two distinct regions ("nearness among worlds"). No duplicate pair.
- `exchangeLaws`: the medium's own laws. Each moves an exact positive `amount` of one `quantity` (`negative` or `positive`) from one endpoint to another. An endpoint is a region, or a world, meaning that world's local sea field for that quantity (`seaCoupling.negativeFieldId` or `positiveFieldId`).
  - region to region requires adjacency;
  - world to region and region to world require the world to be placed in that region;
  - world to world is forbidden, because worlds exchange content only through anchors (Gate B).
  - The guard is a state predicate whose field parameters may bind only endpoint worlds' fields and whose `regionParameters` may bind only endpoint regions' quantities. This keeps the medium local, like the cross-world-read rule of B-034.
- `closure`:
  - `"closed"`: no internal law effect, World-Will intervention effect, or anchor-transfer residue or placement may change a world sea field. The medium is the only source and sink, and every quantity is conserved exactly.
  - `"open"`: such writes are allowed and are recorded as sea production in the balance.

Validation codes for the medium use the `CKW2xx` range in `causal.ts` and `CKR2xx` in the runtime.

## 2. Execution semantics

The medium joins the existing synchronous frontier closure. It adds no clock.

- **Enabled.** At a frontier, an exchange law is enabled when it has not yet occurred in this realization (`once-per-realization`, like internal laws), every endpoint world is active, its guard resolves true, and its source holds at least `amount` (the runtime adds this sufficiency condition itself and records it).
- **Simultaneity.** Enabled exchange laws and enabled internal laws form one frontier.
- **Conflict.**
  - Two exchanges that draw from the same source quantity (the same region and quantity, or the same world sea field) conflict, because shared residue is scarce and one draw per source per frontier is the exact rule.
  - An exchange also conflicts with any other frontier entry that writes the same world sea field, including another exchange that deposits into it: a world boundary admits one medium interaction per quantity per frontier. Two deposits into one region do not conflict; region endpoints are read from the frontier snapshot.
  - Conflicts follow the program's existing `internalConflictMode`: `underdetermined` by default, or every maximal commuting frontier as its own branch with `maximal-commuting-branches`. No host order chooses which exchange draws.
- **Effect.** The source decreases by exactly `amount` and the target increases by exactly `amount`. A changed world sea field recomputes that world's sea viability as before.
- **Event.** Event id `medium:<lawId>`. Its causes are the causes of every bound world field and region quantity. Each region quantity tracks the events that last changed it. The trace entry has kind `sea-medium`. Its effects are world-field effects for world endpoints and `sea-region` effects (with `regionId`, and `fieldId` equal to the quantity) for region endpoints.
- **Release on retirement.** When a world retires under a medium, its entire local negative and positive sea quantities move into its placement region in the same lifecycle frontier, and the world's sea fields become zero. The residue record gains `seaRelease { regionId, negative, positive }`, so collapsed-world residue enters the shared sea.
- **Continuation.** A realization can start from the region quantities and region causes a predecessor left, through the execution options `seaRegionOverrides` (keyed by region id) and `seaRegionOverrideCauses` (keyed by `<regionId>#<quantity>`), exactly as `fieldOverrides` continue world fields. Persistent closures and generated-universe closures use them, and region quantities are part of the configuration identity those layers certify.
- **Non-negative world sea.** Under a medium, a world's coupled sea quantities are amounts of residue or support and can never be negative. A realization in which any world sea field becomes negative, whether by an initializer, an override, or an open-medium writer, is `contradicted`, so a release can never drain the shared sea.
- **Frozen extra sea fields.** Under a closed medium no law, inverse, or intervention may write any sea-role field (CKW233-237), including sea-role fields outside the coupling, which then stay frozen outside the medium's accounting. Generated multi-channel worlds keep their schema channels this way.
- **Transfer certificates.** An exchange that changes a field read by the identity certificate of an anchor transfer cannot share a frontier with that transfer's law, because the certificate is computed on the frontier state. The fields an exchange changes are each world endpoint's exchanged sea field and that world's sea-coupled viability field; the fields a certificate reads are the field parameters of the anchor's identity predicates, in any world. The pair conflicts like any other conflict; an exchange changing nothing the certificate reads may share the frontier.
- **Region event values.** An exchange's region `before` and `after` are taken from the frontier snapshot, so several deposits into one region in one frontier each record the same `before`; the region's realized value after the frontier is the continuation's `seaMedium.regions` entry, and region history is rebuilt from the frontier sequence, not from individual events.
- **Balance.** Every continuation carries a `seaMedium` state: final regions and, for each quantity, `initialTotal`, `finalTotal`, and `production`.
  - Totals sum all regions and the sea fields of all worlds, latent ones included.
  - `production` is the net change of world sea fields caused by events other than exchanges and releases.
  - `balanced` holds exactly when `finalTotal = initialTotal + production`.
  - In a closed medium, `production` must be zero.

## 2a. Generated universes

The endogenous generator places every materialized world in its own region, adjacent to its parent's region, so the sea's topology follows lineage. Exchange templates instantiate once per world with endpoints `self`, `own-region`, and `parent-region`. Region state continues across causal-generation closures and is part of their fixpoint and recurrence identity, and a closed medium contradicts any inter-closure change of the total. A world's medium endpoint is its scalar projection coordinate, so under a medium the run reports `scalarProjection.presentOnlyAtLegacyCausalBoundary: false`: the projection is no longer adapter-only. Continuation state and its causes come only from the generated universe; caller-supplied overrides are discarded.

## 3a. Confirmation audit and release

On 2026-11-17 the author selected confirmation of `v0.5.10` at this scope (D-144). Its closed claim is sections 1 to 3 and the evidence below; section 5 stays open.

Four independent audits (kernel semantics; source syntax, BIR, outcome, and documents; endogenous placement; and a clean-room reader written only from the specifications) found, and this candidate corrected:

- negative world sea quantities let a release drain the shared sea and made joint scarcity a contradiction rather than a typed conflict (B-063);
- an anchor transfer could be admitted on an identity certificate that a same-frontier exchange then falsified (B-064);
- a closed medium did not cover sea-role fields outside the coupling, and runtime sufficiency evidence could collide with an authored predicate id (B-065);
- teleonomic counterfactual reruns carried the medium but not the world lifecycles, the endogenous scalar-projection certificate stayed true under a medium, callers could inject region provenance into a generated universe, and a null medium crashed validation and shared the medium-free identity (B-066);
- the conformance references left the outcome schema, empty-collection rule, nesting rule, minor-version bound, ledger kinds, and several derivation details unstated or stale, and the corpus did not exercise several of them (B-067).

The clean-room reader decodes and re-encodes every one of the twenty-one fixtures byte for byte, recomputes every digest and 101 evidence ids, and produces every program, options, and outcome payload from the generated schema appendix alone.

A re-audit of those corrections found two more, also corrected (D-144):

- the transfer conflict was keyed by endpoint worlds, so an exchange into a third world whose field the identity certificate reads still shared the frontier, while an exchange that changed nothing the certificate reads was refused (B-068);
- a sea medium that is not a declaration object (for example `false`, `0`, an array, or `{}`) crashed kernel validation, and the endogenous guard admitted arrays and incomplete objects (B-069).

Neither correction changes a fixture outcome; the twenty-one fixtures keep their identities and the reference oracle still conforms to all of them.

## 5. Still open

- Persistence: exchange laws are not part of the persistence dependency graph, and self-maintenance counts only negative-sea increases by internal laws, so a closed medium cannot exhibit it.
- Endogenous multi-channel seas: the medium exchanges with the projection coordinate, so schema channels no longer carry a world's exchanged sea; channel-specific exchange and strict `endogenous bubble` medium syntax are open.
- Override causes are not checked against events of the same realization (as for `fieldOverrideCauses`); only layers that tag carried causes with their closure prevent self-causes.
- The corpus does not exercise some schema rows (see causal-conformance.md section 6).

## 6. Not claimed

An anchor transfer always leaves source residue or target placement, so under a closed medium no anchor transfer is admitted; routing crossing residue through the medium is open. Strict `endogenous bubble` source syntax for the medium is open; generated universes declare it in the typed program.

This candidate gives no continuous flow, no repeated diffusion within one realization, no metric distance beyond adjacency, no multi-channel medium in the causal kernel (endogenous channels still project), no general conservation calculus beyond the two quantities, and no Phase 2 observation. It does not replace the scalar viability proxy; it supplies the shared medium that proxy reads through local sea fields.
