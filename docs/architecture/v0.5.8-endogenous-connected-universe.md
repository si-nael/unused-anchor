# v0.5.8 Endogenous Connected-Universe Generation

Recorded: 2026-07-17

Status: completed bounded `v0.5.8` connected-universe generation release; scope selected by the author on 2026-11-16

## Objective

`v0.5.8` must let Bubble law denote and locally realize a connected universe whose worlds, lineage, anchors, boundaries, sea relations, and autonomous continuations are generated from inside one formal structure. It must not be a host loop that appends authored world objects, a random world factory, a catalog of human-scale entities, or a cosmetic wrapper around bounded `spawn-world`.

The current implementation begins the required vertical contract:

1. an intensional constructor denotes a possibly unbounded family of descendant worlds;
2. identity is lineage-relative rather than a cheap absolute multiverse index;
3. every generated relation creates an identity-checked anchor and material positive/negative sea action, and may derive exact endpoint transport when its constructor law requires payload continuity;
4. a finite host materialization preserves every frontier beyond its demand instead of claiming that the universe ends there;
5. every materialized world is lowered into the existing actor-neutral causal kernel and continues through its own local laws;
6. exact false admissions remain dormant constructor frontiers and are reevaluated after causal closure, so realized state can generate further worlds;
7. every lawful plural causal continuation becomes its own lineage-bearing generated-universe branch and continues independently;
8. every finite internal demand yields a deterministic complete result form while leaving the infinite denotation and every unresolved frontier intact;
9. each constructor may inherit the parent's realized schema revision, replace one complete executable world-local schema graph as a single typed object, then idempotently remove or upsert complete internal laws and intensionally specialize irreversible-law effect values for its child;
10. inspection and replay preserve the generated topology, schema-revision lineage, sea channels, causal provenance, branch/closure sequence, self-complementing evidence chain, and absence of host selection.
11. one strict `endogenous bubble` source profile denotes that same formal system, complete schema graph, constructors, relations, seas, transformations, and clockless order without a JSON-only semantic branch or executable host-text escape hatch.

## 1. Coinductive Generation Structure

Let `S` be a finite set of typed world schemas and `C` a finite set of constructors. A constructor

$$
c : s_p \times I_c \rightharpoonup s_c
$$

has a parent schema $s_p$, child schema $s_c$, local coordinate domain $I_c$, and an exact Boolean admission predicate. $I_c$ may be finite or countably infinite. Recursive schema incidence makes the denoted universe unbounded even when every individual constructor has finite arity.

The resulting world family has the polynomial-coalgebra shape

$$
\mathcal G(X) = R + \sum_{c \in C} A_c \times X^{I_c},
$$

where $R$ contains root seeds and $A_c$ contains the exact constructor, identity, anchor, and sea data attached to one generated relation. The implementation does not claim a computed final coalgebra. It stores the finite law and materializes a finite demanded subcoalgebra with explicit continuation frontiers.

## 2. Lineage-Relative Identity

A generated world address is

$$
a = \bigl(r; (c_1,i_1), \ldots, (c_n,i_n)\bigr),
$$

where $r$ is a root identity and every $(c_k,i_k)$ is a constructor and its coordinate relative to the previous world. Runtime identifiers are deterministic digests of this full address. The digest is storage identity; the address is semantic provenance.

No global breadth-first ordinal names the world's absolute place in the universe. Two materializers that demand the same lineage address derive the same world identity regardless of traversal order.

## 3. Anchor And Sea Relation

Every world receives a local identity anchor. Every admitted parent-child construction also produces a relation anchor

$$
A_c : W_p \leftrightarrow W_c
$$

with explicit ports and an exact identity predicate over endpoint fields.

Sea structure is channel-valued. For each world $W$, let

$$
S_W^+ = \prod_{k \in K_W} V_k^+, \qquad
S_W^- = \prod_{k \in K_W} V_k^-,
$$

where channels may distinguish lineage support, boundary support, placement, residue, erosion pressure, or later author-derived structural directions without collapsing them into one moral or biological score.

A generated relation applies exact actions

$$
\Delta^-_{c,k} \in V^-_{p,k}, \qquad
\Delta^+_{c,k} \in V^+_{c,k}.
$$

Sibling relations are proposed from one parent snapshot and their additive sea actions are accumulated commutatively. Declaration order cannot change admission or select one sibling.

The same relation may carry an exact schema morphism

$$
T_c : F_{p,j} \longrightarrow F_{c,k},
$$

from a non-sea parent payload field into an unprotected child condition field of the same exact value kind. Every sibling transport reads the common pre-generation parent snapshot. The transport is recorded on the generated anchor, carries `hostSelection: false`, and supplies causal provenance to the receiving world's later local law. It cannot write identity, protected structure, memory, boundary state, or a sea coordinate directly. Sea residue and placement remain explicit channel actions rather than being hidden inside the copied value.

The older causal membrane kernel now also admits multiple crossings only when a commuting frontier can be derived mechanically: target fields are unique, no crossing target becomes another crossing's source in the same frontier, and other crossings cannot mutate an anchor-identity dependency. Payloads are read from one pre-frontier snapshot, target substitutions are atomic, and shared sea effects compose by exact addition. Chained transport therefore requires another causal frontier instead of gaining an accidental host order.

## 4. Inherited Executable Schema-Graph Revisions

Generated worlds no longer execute only the host-authored static schema table. Each root begins with a content-addressed base schema revision. A same-schema child without a transformation inherits its parent's exact revision; a cross-schema child begins from the child schema's base revision. One executable world-local schema graph is

$$
\Sigma=(F,P,O,S,I,L,E,\Pi),
$$

where $F$ is the typed field-and-initializer set, $P$ the protected subset, $O$ the anchor ports, $S$ the named positive/negative sea channels, $I$ the self-identity predicate, $L$ the internal-law set, $E$ the emergence criteria, and $\Pi$ the optional causal compatibility projection. These components are one dependency graph: a protected field, sea endpoint, predicate binding, law effect, emergence witness, or projection target is valid only when its referenced field or channel exists with the required exact kind.

A constructor may carry a complete typed graph morphism

$$
\mu_{c,a}: \Sigma_p \longrightarrow \Sigma_{c,a},
$$

where $a$ is the child's lineage address. `replaceGraph` supplies the whole target graph rather than independent category flags. The target is normalized and content-addressed before the child exists; only then are its field initializers evaluated. Therefore child fields, self-anchor, relation endpoint, sea actions, causal compiler, local laws, emergence criteria, and World-Will compatibility objective all consume the same realized revision. No consumer may silently reload one component from the base schema.

The replacement is recursively closed: applying the same constructor to a descendant replaces the inherited graph with the same complete target graph rather than attempting a one-use field or port migration. Every realized event records source and target graph digests, whether total replacement occurred, and the exact component set whose content changed. Prior worlds retain their prior revisions.

After the graph step, the constructor may carry a typed law-set and effect-value morphism

$$
\lambda_{c,a}: L_p \longrightarrow L_{c,a},
$$

Each transformed operand is the result of an exact intensional query whose sources may depend on root seed, lineage depth, local coordinate, and the parent snapshot. The query result must have the exact value kind of the target field. The runtime records the source revision, graph change, evaluated query, exact result, proof digest, constructor, transformation, target revision, and child world as one causal provenance chain with `hostSelection: false`.

This is an inheritable schema-revision morphism, not arbitrary self-modifying code. `removeLawIds` removes inherited base-law identities idempotently. `upsertLaws` supplies a complete typed law definition: it adds the law when absent and replaces the inherited definition when present, so the same constructor morphism remains closed under recursive application instead of failing after its first generation. A complete upsert may change guard, effects, application-compatible definition, reversibility, inverse effects, and explicit commits only after the ordinary child-field, predicate, effect-kind, same-law duplicate-field, inverse, and commit contracts validate. A reversible upsert must carry its whole exact additive inverse contract. The separate lineage-relative `effectValues` operation remains limited to irreversible laws because changing only a forward operand would break a reversible pair.

The complete graph replacement now changes fields and their roles/initializers, protected sets, ports, self-identity, sea channels, internal laws, emergence criteria, and the optional causal projection together. Static validation closes all references and exact kinds before any world exists, including recursive parent compatibility for admission, relation bindings, transports, ports, and sea actions. Invalid protected fields, witnesses, channels, inverse laws, commits, or endpoint dependencies contradict the program rather than producing a partial child.

The morphism still cannot change constructors, schema incidence, or intensional family definitions. It also cannot generate or execute source code. Host callbacks and executable strings are absent. `graphChange` binds source/target graph identity and changed components; `lawSetChange` separately records requested and effective removals, upserts, actual additions and replacements, and exact source/target law-ID sets at every generation.

All admitted siblings are prepared from the same parent revision and snapshot. If any required transformation is unresolved within its exact query budget, no sibling world or revision is committed and every sibling coordinate is retained as a `schema-transformation-undetermined` frontier. Compilation reads each world's realized revision rather than reloading `schema.internalLaws`. Recurrence identity includes the full realized revision, so equal fields cannot hide changed law structure. Inspection and replay preserve every transformation event and reject revision-provenance tampering.

## 4.5. Lossless Declarative Source Boundary

The strict `endogenous bubble` profile is an authoring projection of `EndogenousUniverseProgram`, not another world kernel. Its intensional expressions cover exact literals, parameters, axes, unary and binary algebra, conditionals, family-coordinate references, and guarded natural recursion. Its graph declarations cover the complete executable schema object; constructor declarations cover admission, relation identity, transport, sea action, and schema morphism. The explicit causal-partial/no-clock declaration is mandatory.

The compiler lowers the canonical source to an object structurally identical to the retained JSON fixture, then invokes the same endogenous validator. Run, inspection, record, and replay CLIs accept either projection; the canonical evidence cycle now starts from `.bubble`. A source-level complete replacement graph is tested independently from the JSON/programmatic graph fixture. Universal-clock requests, incomplete graphs, dangling references, ill-typed family results, and invalid exact contracts fail before world materialization.

The source cannot generate or execute implementation code. It also does not make a finitely declared graph an intensionally invented graph. Those remain different later contracts.

## 5. Scalar Projection Is Not Ontology

The legacy anchored-causal executor still requires one positive scalar, negative scalar, and `viability` coordinate. A schema may therefore provide an optional compatibility map

$$
\nu_W(S_W^+,S_W^-) = v_0 + \sum_k \alpha_k S^+_{W,k} - \sum_k \beta_k S^-_{W,k}.
$$

This map is generated only at the boundary to the old executor. It is not stored as one of the schema's ontological sea channels, and a universe without $\nu_W$ remains validly materializable. It becomes `undetermined` only when asked to execute through that legacy backend. This directly prevents the B-038 scalar proxy from becoming universal worldhood.

## 6. Finite Demand Without False Finitude

Execution options bound depth, total materialized worlds, natural-coordinate prefix, and formal evaluation work.

- A depth boundary is a resolved finite view with a preserved latent continuation frontier.
- A natural-coordinate limit preserves an `natural-open` frontier; it does not assert that later coordinates are rejected.
- An unresolved admission predicate remains `undetermined` at that coordinate. Every sibling of the same family whose admission did resolve true is kept as a `sibling-admission-pending` frontier rather than materialized or forgotten.
- Generation proceeds one lineage depth at a time. If the world budget cannot contain every admitted sibling family of a whole level, no member of that level is materialized and every proposal becomes a `world-budget` frontier; later levels are recorded but not materialized. Neither coordinate nor world-identifier order can decide which cousin family survives. The host may not select a convenient prefix and call it the generated world.
- If the world budget is below the root set, no root is materialized and each root becomes a `root-world-budget` frontier.
- `exhaustiveUniverse` is true only for a resolved, complete demanded slice with no remaining frontier. Recursive canonical worlds therefore correctly report false.

Resource order is not world time. The semantic order is root or parent world, constructor admission, generation event, child world, generated anchor/sea relation, and later child-local causal events.

## 7. Infinite Denotation, Finite Internal Cut

Let `P` be one typed endogenous-universe program and let

$$
\delta = (d, w, n, e, c, b)
$$

be a finite internal demand over lineage depth, world count, natural-coordinate prefix, query work, causal-generation closures, and complete branch-family capacity. The demanded realization is a deterministic semantic map

$$
D_{\delta}(P) \longrightarrow
\{R_{1}, R_{*}, \partial, \bot\},
$$

where $R_1$ is one closed result, $R_*$ is the complete closed lawful branch family, $\partial$ is an explicit open boundary, and $\bot$ is contradiction. Determinism means that the same `P` and $\delta$ produce the same complete result and evidence. It does not mean that the host selects one branch. A plural $R_*$ is one determined answer.

The program may still denote an unbounded structure through a natural-coordinate constructor or recursive schema incidence. The certificate therefore separates `potentially-unbounded` denotation from finite materialization and explicitly preserves `infinityRemainsIntensional`. Closing $D_{\delta}(P)$ proves only closure for $\delta$; it never proves that the entire coinductive universe has been executed.

Repeated realization builds a cryptographic evidence chain

$$
E_{k+1}=H(E_k,C_k),
$$

where $C_k$ contains the closure input, every complete causal outcome, generated events, branch derivation, and fixpoint/open evidence. The chain may also show that realized local state reactivated an `admission-closed` constructor. This is the implemented meaning of self-complementing realization: causal-generation evidence accumulates monotonically and internal law may discharge or reopen a previously recorded boundary.

It is not a monotonicity claim about world fields. A world may reverse or recur. Exact active recurrence now has a separate bounded identity-and-anchor certificate, but that certificate is not a general convergence theorem, an actual-infinity supertask, whole-universe completion, or external observation. Phase 2 observation and perspective-relative collapse remain separate.

## 8. Autonomous World Continuation

Every schema contains exact local field initializers, identity predicates, local causal laws, protected structure, optional emergence criteria, and its structural sea channels. Once a finite slice is derived, each instance is namespaced and lowered into one shared actor-neutral causal program.

The generated program has:

- no protagonist, story, agent, teleonomic label, or observer requirement;
- no World-Will intervention or host-selected delivery in the canonical path;
- one World-Will objective per world only because the current causal validator requires an explicit governing orientation;
- all simultaneously admitted local laws evaluated by existing exact commuting-frontier semantics;
- generated relation anchors and identity predicates present in the same causal system;
- generated transport provenance retained as the cause of the receiving condition and therefore of any law whose guard reads it;
- every generated world accompanied by continuation evidence and `hostSelection: false`.

The optional compatibility projection does not authorize World Will to write protected structure. Local world law remains the only producer of the canonical structural condensation.

Generation and local law now form a bounded closure coalgebra:

$$
M_n \xrightarrow{\text{causal closure}} C_n
\xrightarrow{\text{constructor admission}} M_{n+1}.
$$

An exactly false admission is recorded as `admission-closed`, not deleted from the intensional law. After the unique causal continuation changes world fields, the same constructor coordinate is reevaluated from that realized state. Existing lineage addresses are never regenerated and prior relation sea actions are not applied twice. Newly admitted siblings are still committed as a complete set, then receive their own causal closure. A closure limit that ends after generation but before those worlds continue reports `undetermined`.

Each closure stores digests of its complete input and output demanded configurations and its realized-event count. No new world is only topology stability. A closure fixpoint additionally requires identical configuration digests and zero realized events, so the canonical condensation path needs a second closure to prove that its post-condensation state remains unchanged. Returning to the same fields through realized events is recurrence, not a fixed point.

For a repeated causal-generation configuration $Q_i=Q_j$ with activity, the runtime now derives a generated-recurrence certificate only if

$$
L_i=L_j,\quad A_i=A_j,\quad G_i=G_j,
$$

where $L$ is lineage/world structure, $A$ is the complete identity/relation-anchor set, and $G$ is the generation-event set; every exact anchor-identity predicate holds at both cycle cuts; and

$$
\operatorname{Mut}(E_{i:j})\cap\operatorname{Dep}_{identity}=\varnothing.
$$

Thus an identity dependency may not change inside the proposed cycle even if later events restore its final value. Identity proof exhaustion remains `undetermined`, and a false predicate or mutated dependency leaves recurrence unclosed. A certified recurrence closes the finite demand as `persistent-recurrence`, while `fixpointReached` remains false.

The certificate keeps three different histories separate:

- generated worlds, generation events, and anchors are world structure and must be preserved exactly across the repeated configuration;
- causal-event occurrences form monotone replay evidence proving that activity occurred;
- explicit history commits or generation events extend internal world history.

Therefore replay evidence may grow while `worldHistoryExtended` is false and `fullAnchoredStateRepeated` is true. A commit-bearing recurrence instead records world-history extension and a non-repeated full anchored state. This prevents the self-complementing evidence ledger from impersonating internal Bubble history.

If one causal closure has several lawful continuations, each continuation induces a branch step

$$
b_{n+1} = (b_n, C_n, \kappa_i),
$$

where $\kappa_i$ is one causally derived continuation, not a host choice. Each branch carries its own materialization, configuration history, generated anchors and seas, closure evidence, and later branch family. Equal field configurations are not merged merely because their current projection matches: different causal or anchor histories remain different until an explicit identity theorem proves otherwise.

`maxGenerationBranches` bounds the complete next branch family. The runtime computes the required family cardinality before continuing any member. If the family exceeds the bound, it preserves every continuation as one open branch frontier on the common input and materializes none of the competing subset. Declaration or traversal order therefore cannot decide which universe survives a resource limit.

Query templates are also closed statically before materialization. Family axes must match exactly, direct sources and field bindings must provide each declared parameter exactly once, exact value kinds must agree, finite literal coordinates must lie in their domains, reachable root seeds must supply a stable value, and `parent-field` sources are legal only where a parent snapshot necessarily exists. A malformed query cannot quietly turn into a false admission or a dormant world.

The same total contract now applies to schema-graph and law-revision morphisms. A total replacement must itself differ from the base graph unless another material law operation exists. Field initializers, ports, protected fields, sea endpoints, self-identity bindings, emergence witnesses, and projection channels/targets must form one closed exact graph. Law removals must identify stable target child laws; removal and upsert targets cannot overlap; upsert IDs and affected fields must be unique; each upsert guard is a total Boolean child-local query; effect values and operations must fit target-revision field kinds; commits must name target fields; and reversible upserts need exact paired inverses. A later effect-value specialization must identify one law in the post-upsert target set exactly once, use an in-range effect index, target an irreversible law, and produce the affected field's exact kind. Any malformed, duplicate, reversible-forward-only, or structurally unknown target contradicts the program before any world exists.

## 9. Replay Identity

The replay record stores the generative program, finite demand, full materialization, generated topology, exact channel values, every causal-generation closure, and stable digests. Replay verifies:

- source-program digest;
- stored-run digest;
- deterministic lineage addresses and world identifiers;
- generation events and relation anchors;
- sea channel actions and frontiers;
- causal program result and every lawfully preserved autonomous continuation;
- the finite-demand result form and every digest-linked self-complementation step.
- every generated-recurrence identity, anchor, causal-evidence, and committed-history certificate.

Tampering with a generated identity field, frontier, anchor, sea value, or causal continuation invalidates integrity or produces reexecution drift.

## Release Confirmation

On 2026-11-16 the author selected confirmation of `v0.5.8` at the scope recorded in Current Closed Evidence rather than widening it further. The confirmed claim is exactly that closed vertical evidence: strict endogenous source, one typed intensional/constructor/schema program, complete sibling generation, inherited and wholly replaceable executable schema graphs, lineage-relative identity, identity and relation anchors, exact channel-valued sea action and parent-to-child transport, receiver-local law response, realized-state constructor reactivation, recursively branch-indexed generated universes, the finite internal-demand certificate, digest-linked self-complementation evidence, demanded fixpoint or exact persistent recurrence, and integrity-checked inspection and replay. An independent adversarial audit preceded confirmation. It found and the release corrected three false surfaces inside this scope: constructor endpoints were not closed against revisions produced by other constructors (B-050); world budgets could select an order-dependent root or cousin prefix and still claim an exhaustive universe (B-051); and an admitted sibling could vanish from the frontier when another sibling's admission was unresolved (B-052). It also narrowed one evidence claim about source-level graph replacement (B-049) and left two identity semantics for author judgment (B-053). The confirmation reran TypeScript checking, every unit test, and the complete artifact verification pipeline, and reconciled package, obligation, question, hypothesis, implementation-map, reference, README, and memory records (D-133).

Confirmation narrows nothing below and promotes no item below into a closed claim. It does not complete OB-001, select the content of a next patch version, or authorize Phase 2.

## Remaining Open After Bounded v0.5.8

This release is a large bounded beginning, not the complete connected Bubble universe. The following remain open:

- intensional generation of new schema graphs rather than complete typed graph replacement, plus transformation of constructors, schema incidence, and intensional family definitions;
- repeated post-generation membrane crossing and dependency-ordered chained transport beyond the now-closed simultaneous exact sibling frontier;
- topology-changing, quotient, non-periodic, or coinductive persistence beyond exact repeated finite configurations; the bounded certificate does not prove every generated component's self-maintenance;
- a general theorem of self-complementing convergence beyond the implemented monotone evidence ledger;
- branch equivalence, lawful convergence, and constructor-relation conflict/commutativity beyond the now-closed finite branch-indexed continuation baseline;
- stronger anchor identity calculus and non-scalar structural support operators;
- two identity semantics found by the confirmation audit and left for author judgment (B-053): a `parent-field` source inside a relation-identity predicate is a birth snapshot, so it is a constant rather than a live identity dependency and a recurrence certificate does not watch it; and a demanded fixpoint is certified by configuration equality and zero realized events without evaluating anchor identity at the final cut, unlike `persistent-recurrence`, so a fixpoint can be reported while a generated anchor's identity predicate is false;
- source-level executable evidence for a graph replacement that actually changes the graph and for evaluation of a recursive family declared in source (B-049);
- the portable BIR semantic graph (Gate C) and implementation-independent conformance plus host-cost evidence (Gate D) before the Rust `v0.6.x` transition.

Phase 2 observers, human-scale classification, recursive implementation self-construction, and trans-Turing claims remain outside this release boundary.
