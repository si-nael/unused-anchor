# Necessity-First Bubble Core

Recorded: 2026-07-17

Status: binding philosophical and architectural baseline for work after `v0.5.7`

## Why This Baseline Exists

Commit `00bbdf7` (`v0.4.7`) preserves the clearest pre-assistant form of two different things that later work sometimes mixed:

1. an observer-free account of worldhood in which internal law, exterior openness, structural situatedness, and replay identity force distinct sea and anchor roles;
2. human-facing research examples involving observers, agents, protagonists, and stories.

The author's later clarification resolves the ambiguity. The first is core Bubble ontology. The second may describe or probe some worlds, but it is neither the substance of a Bubble nor a completion condition for the universe.

This does not make every sentence at `v0.4.7` equally authoritative. The controlling sources are the observer-free necessity argument in [Sea-Anchor Necessity Proof](sea-anchor-proof.md), the working ontology in [Sea-Anchor Model](sea-anchor-model.md), the universal-semantic-unit and universe-generating-language ideas in the dated idea record, and the author's later corrections preserved in the author corpus.

## Core Authority

Bubble begins from structures that are necessary to the author's intended world, not from a catalog of familiar things to place inside it.

- A Bubble is the universal semantic unit, not one application object beside hidden non-Bubble machinery.
- A world has its own internal law and is not a copy of a privileged original.
- Openness to admissible exterior pressure supplies the negative-sea role.
- Situated continuation, lineage, placement, and support supply the positive-sea role.
- Same-world identity across lawful disturbance and replay supplies the anchor role.
- Boundary, causal dependence, transformation, lineage, memory, commitment, and relation are represented at the world-structural level.
- World Will is an internal governing orientation constrained by the world's formal structure. It is not a host command, convenient selector, or name for unexplained choice.
- No universal observer, protagonist, scalar clock, narrative, moral polarity, or host scheduler is required for worldhood.

The analytic proof establishes necessity only inside its stated model. It does not license claims of metaphysical proof, and its path parameter does not install universal time.

## Neutral Mechanisms Preserved From Later Work

Later versions produced mechanisms that remain useful because their executable content is observer-independent:

- exact intensional denotation and demand-bounded evaluation;
- state-bound causal laws and exact closure;
- preservation of every lawfully generated non-commuting continuation;
- recurrence and persistence evidence separated from committed history;
- world actualization and cessation with monotone provenance and retained residue;
- typed anchor-mediated cross-world effects with explicit sea consequences;
- inspection, source attribution, record integrity, and exact replay.

Their mathematical contracts may remain even when their original presentation used an overly familiar name. In particular, `birth`, `retirement`, and `lifecycle` are current interface vocabulary for actualization, cessation, and phase provenance; they are not claims that a Bubble is biologically alive.

## Interpretive Layers, Not Core Ontology

The following preserved implementations are compatibility and research projections:

- `narrative.ts` and `narrative-runtime.ts` encode the bounded `v0.5.1` protagonist/story experiment;
- `teleonomic-runtime.ts` interprets neutral invariant-set, response-necessity, and memory-counterfactual evidence through the historical `v0.5.4` terminology;
- observer, agent, life, being, relationship, protagonist, goal, affordance, and story labels are perspective-relative descriptions when separately justified.

These modules remain available through the explicit `world-kernel.interpretive` namespace and by their historical direct paths. They are not exported as peers of the observer-independent core. No present or future core module may import them, and no release may require one of their labels to certify Bubble worldhood.

Historical code and release records are not deleted or rewritten: they show what was actually tested and why later correction was necessary. Preservation is not promotion.

## Remaining Core Debt Found By The Audit

Separating interpretation modules repairs one architectural boundary; it does not prove that every remaining core type is necessary.

- Every causal world currently requires one rational `viabilityFieldId`, and the runtime adds weighted positive- and negative-sea scalars into it. This is a bounded executable proxy, not the general sea action or support structure derived by the `v0.4.7` model.
- The persistence detector currently privileges a fixed bundle of `structural-state`, `identity-state`, `memory-state`, and `boundary-state` roles. Those roles support one falsifiable detector but are not a universal decomposition theorem for every persistent Bubble.
- World-Will interventions use a closed host-authored kind list. Its exact objective and anchor checks are useful, but the taxonomy is not yet internally generated Bubble structure.
- Actualization and cessation are implemented only over finitely predeclared worlds. The host still supplies the eventual field and law schemas.
- `birth`, `retirement`, `lifecycle`, `viability`, `teleonomic`, and `affordance` remain in historical interfaces. Their compatibility presence must never be confused with ontological privilege.

These findings stay open. Removing the fields or renaming them without a replacement formal contract would only hide the debt. Before or inside the `v0.5.8` connected-universe seam, mandatory scalar viability must become an optional derived observation over a more general structural sea relation, persistence roles must remain detector parameters rather than definitions of existence, and generated world structure must cease to depend on a host-authored population list.

Status at bounded `v0.5.8` (recorded 2026-11-16): constructor law, not a host population list, derives every generated world. The generator materializes worlds without a scalar viability coordinate and keeps the scalar map only as an optional compatibility projection outside its ontological sea channels. Its recurrence certificate uses lineage, schema revision, and anchor identity and does not import the fixed persistence-role detector. Generated worlds still execute through the legacy anchored-causal executor, which requires that scalar projection, so the first condition is met for worldhood and materialization but not for execution. The legacy persistence detector, the closed World-Will intervention taxonomy, and predeclared lifecycle schemas outside the generator are unchanged and remain open debt.

## Necessity Gate For New Core Concepts

A new primitive or privileged derived class may enter the core only if all of the following are answered:

1. Which recorded author concept or stated worldhood axiom requires it?
2. What contradiction, loss of identity, loss of situatedness, or loss of lawful continuation follows if it is absent?
3. Can its executable contract be stated without an observer or a human-scale analogy?
4. Is it generated or constrained by Bubble law rather than selected by host convenience?
5. Are its provenance, failure boundary, underdetermination, and replay meaning explicit?

If those questions are not satisfied, the construct remains an experiment, interpretation, tool surface, or open question. Passing tests alone cannot promote it into ontology.

## Consequence For v0.5.8

The connected-universe generator must depend only on the observer-independent core. It must derive typed worlds, local law structure, lineage, boundaries, anchor relations, sea placement or residue, and lawful continuation without requiring a protagonist, story, teleonomic label, observer, or host-authored population list.

Generated structure may later admit many interpretations. The generator succeeds because the connected Bubble whole becomes necessary from its own law, not because the runtime recognizes a familiar human pattern in it.
