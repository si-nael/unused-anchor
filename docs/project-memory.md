# Project Memory

Last updated: 2026-11-17

## Purpose

This file stores durable cross-session facts needed to continue the project without reinterpreting its foundation. It is intentionally concise. Raw ideas, research questions, hypotheses, decisions, completed work, and boundary failures remain in their specialized logs.

## Core Aim

Build a research-grade language and runtime for coherent independent bubble universes. Each bubble can carry its own laws, history, boundary, lineage, world will, and anchor while remaining intensionally represented until observation, interaction, proof, replay, or commitment requires a concrete slice.

The target is not decorative cosmological vocabulary. The runtime should eventually let finite observers and tools distinguish lawful internal events, outside residue or erosion, lineage and continuity shifts, and failures of same-world identity.

## Philosophical Authority

The binding baseline is the observer-free core preserved at commit `00bbdf7` (`v0.4.7`): a Bubble has its own law, and openness, situatedness, and replay identity force distinct negative-sea, positive-sea, and anchor roles inside the stated model. The agent, protagonist, and story passages also present in that revision are explanatory research viewpoints, not peer ontology. Later exact causal, intensional, persistence, branching, actualization, membrane, provenance, and replay mechanisms remain valuable where their contracts are observer-independent.

The public world kernel therefore exposes human-scale narrative and teleonomic experiments only under an explicit `interpretive` namespace. Historical code and release evidence remain preserved, but no core module, release goal, or universe-completion criterion may depend on those labels. New primitives must pass the necessity gate in [architecture/necessity-first-bubble-core.md](architecture/necessity-first-bubble-core.md).

This boundary repair is not full philosophical completion. The legacy causal adapter still requires a scalar `viabilityFieldId`, the persistence detector uses one fixed field-role bundle, and World Will uses a closed intervention-kind taxonomy. The active connected generator can now replace one complete executable world-local schema graph, but it still cannot intensionally generate a new graph or formal family, transform constructor/schema incidence, or prove that this finite graph vocabulary is universal Bubble ontology. These remain bounded contracts and must not be promoted into philosophical completion.

## Stable Vocabulary

- **Bubble universe:** an independent world, not a lower-status copy of one privileged original; it has its own law, history, boundary, lineage, and identity conditions.
- **Negative sea:** the directionally dissolving outer field associated with erosion, outside residue, leakage, boundary shaking, failed-law fragments, and unsecured records. It is not moral evil or empty space.
- **Positive sea:** the directionally structuring outer field associated with lineage, placement, continuity, relation, growth, and stabilization. It is not moral good and is wider than an anchor.
- **Anchor point:** the identity fixpoint that explains why one bubble remains this world across disturbance, observation, commitment, replay, and growth. It binds law basis, committed history, trusted observation, and boundary coherence.
- **World will:** an authored governing orientation. It is executable only when authored as a supported criterion; quoted prose remains explicit descriptive intent rather than fake executable law.
- **World self-realization:** the bubble derives lawful continuation candidates from its own state and transformations, then its executable world will admits them. External tooling may inspect or resume a provenance-bearing continuation but must not choose a candidate secretly.
- **Protagonist:** a human-scale observer projection that may be applied to some persistent internal organization; it is not Bubble ontology, mandatory schema, a privileged emergence class, or a release goal.
- **Story:** a human-scale projection over some causal history; it is not a prose field, primitive world substance, required outcome, or universal way to organize Bubble history.
- **Life/being/relationship:** perspective-relative names that must not be installed as privileged Bubble primitives or success criteria. The kernel implements deeper identity, persistence, relation, memory, boundary, causality, and generation without deciding in advance which human label applies.
- **Formal intensional family:** a finite typed definition of an indexed object `f : I -> V`. It may denote countably infinite or infinite-dimensional structure while materializing only demanded coordinates.
- **Computability boundary:** world truth or denotation, causal accessibility to an internal being, and effective host evaluation are distinct. The current runtime raises the infinite representability frontier but does not claim hypercomputation. Any future oracle-relative, limit-derived, axiomatic, exact-continuum, or physical non-Turing result must record the extra source and use an appropriate replay contract.
- **BIR:** the Bubble Intermediate Representation, a canonical machine-independent semantic graph with semantic identity over critical sections, storage identity over all bytes, and the host object digest as a separate backend identity. Its bytes are a projection of Bubble meaning, not the world itself.
- **Canonical string order:** Unicode code-point order (bytewise UTF-8), never host locale collation.
- **Causal conformance:** exact equality of a backend's portable causal outcome with a frozen fixture's expected outcome; host cost is measured but never compared.
- **Execution substrate boundary:** Bubble meaning is a typed machine-independent semantic relation, not TypeScript objects, JSON text, Rust memory, one binary file, or a host evaluation order. TypeScript remains the current reference oracle; late 5.x must establish a portable semantic graph and conformance/cost evidence before Rust becomes the first native backend in 6.x.
- **Time/order:** Bubble assumes no universal clock. A world may be invariant, reversible, cyclic, causally or partially ordered, branching, terminal, or history-directed. Explicit commits create committed history; bounded `v0.5.6` separately treats durably preserved birth or retirement provenance as a world-lifecycle history-arrow source without inventing duration.
- **Observation:** bounded causal contact, not automatically a passive complete read. A bubble may persist as compressed lawful existence before contact.
- **Persistent causal structure:** a recurrent component derived from conjunctive law dependencies whose boundary-mediated hypercuts, invariant identity, universally effective retained-memory counterfactual, recurrent-reference restoration, and outward influence all have exact evidence. Configuration recurrence is kept separate from monotone committed anchor history. It is not automatically alive, agentic, conscious, a protagonist, or a story.
- **Historical teleonomic interpretation:** the `v0.5.4` name applied to neutral invariant-set, response-necessity, memory-dependence, and autonomy evidence. The mathematics remains usable, but `teleonomic capacity` and `affordance` are interpretive vocabulary rather than Bubble ontology or a required stage toward agency or life.
- **Endogenous causal branch:** one actual continuation derived from a maximal commuting frontier of simultaneously enabled internal laws. Every maximal frontier is preserved, excluded conflicting laws are recorded as nonrealized in that branch, and no host or World-Will selector chooses between branch outcomes. It is world plurality, not yet deliberation by an internal being.
- **Bounded world lifecycle:** a declared latent world's causal actualization by active-world internal law, or an active world's retirement by its own law. Birth carries parent/cause lineage and retirement retains final fields, local negative-sea residue, and committed memory. It is distinct from observation materialization and branch nonrealization and does not mean dynamic schema or population generation.
- **Typed anchor membrane candidate:** one directed exact relation between ports on two active worlds. A source-world internal law may copy one exact snapshot into an unprotected receiving condition only when anchor identity holds before and after crossing; the crossing leaves source negative-sea residue and target positive-sea placement, after which the receiving world can respond only through its own law. It is not a host router, direct protected-state write, general transport calculus, or global sea.
- **Endogenous connected-universe candidate:** a finite intensional constructor law denotes a recursively connected family of lineage-relative world instances. A finite demand materializes a complete local sibling set, identity and relation anchors, channel-valued sea actions, and any exact parent-to-child condition transport while preserving every unvisited frontier. Generated transport provenance can cause the receiving world's local law. This is not a host-created object catalog, arbitrary schema invention, or an exhaustive infinite universe; bounded `v0.5.8` confirms only its recorded finite evidence.
- **Inherited executable schema revision:** the content-addressed world-local graph actually used by one materialized world: fields and initializers, protected set, ports, sea channels, self-identity, internal laws, emergence criteria, and optional causal projection. A same-schema descendant inherits its parent's realized revision. One recursively closed constructor may replace the whole graph atomically, then remove/upsert complete laws and specialize irreversible-law effects. Source/target graph identity, changed components, exact proof, child, relation, recurrence identity, inspection, and replay remain bound. This is not intensional graph/family generation, arbitrary code execution, or successor-Bubble self-construction.
- **Finite internal realization and self-complementation:** the same finite program and demand deterministically yield one closed result, the complete closed branch family, an open boundary, or contradiction. The complete plurality is one answer and no host branch is selected. Closure evidence forms a predecessor-digest chain and can record dormant-law reactivation, but this does not make world state monotone, guarantee convergence, complete infinity, or introduce a Phase 2 observer.
- **Generated persistent recurrence:** an active repeated finite generated configuration is not a fixpoint. It closes only when lineage/world structure, generation provenance, every identity/relation anchor, and exact anchor predicates persist and no event in the cycle mutates an identity dependency. Causal replay evidence and internal history commits are counted separately; evidence growth alone cannot create world history or prove component self-maintenance.

The canonical ontology is [architecture/sea-anchor-model.md](architecture/sea-anchor-model.md). These definitions remain a working ontology and must not be claimed as a perfect substitute for unrecorded user intent.

## Research Preservation

The original dated ideas remain authoritative context even when one bounded implementation slice is completed. A completed baseline does not mean the motivating idea has been exhausted.

- retain original idea text and later add status, evidence, limits, and reopen conditions around it
- preserve partial, open, and deferred questions in the implementation map
- interpret a release non-goal only as a scope boundary for that release
- do not use runtime proxies to declare the full sea, anchor, observer, replay, or probabilistic research programs complete
- do not relabel infinite denotation, unbounded generation, branching, randomness, World Will, anchors, or self-reference as hypercomputation without typed non-effective provenance
- do not equate TypeScript JSON, canonical BIR bytes, Rust memory, CPU scheduling, or faster output with Bubble semantic identity
- close one connected objective per `v0.5.x` release without erasing the other branches

## Honesty Rules

- Keep authored intent, inferred runtime assessment, materialized evidence, and mathematical proof distinct.
- Do not label a bounded score or proxy as the completed ontology.
- Do not invent executed causal history for effects that remain potential.
- Do not claim same-world replay merely because a stored report can be reopened.
- Do not claim persistence from recurrence, field-role names, or stable scores alone; require causal boundary, identity, memory, maintenance, and influence evidence.
- Date new records with the actual recording date.
- Natural language may preserve intent but must not silently become executable law; executable semantics require typed formal lowering.
- When evidence cannot distinguish an internal event, negative-sea pressure, anchor drift, or positive-sea shift, record the source as unresolved.
